I am **Jun Zhu**, a PhD student in Statistics at **Renmin University of China**. My research broadly focuses on **statistical machine learning**, with current interests in **optimal transport**, **sufficient dimension reduction**, **representation learning**, and **data-driven decision-making**.

My recent work explores topics such as:

- optimal transport and its applications to classification, clustering, and representation learning;
- sufficient dimension reduction for complex and structured data;
- time-series representation and feature extraction;
- graph representation, graph coarsening, and curvature-based methods;
- decision learning and policy evaluation.
